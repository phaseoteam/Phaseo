The app opens on a new chat, and the bottom tab bar is gone. A sidebar (a drawer on phones, always open on tablets) holds search and New chat, a Chat | Code switch between Phaseo chats on the phone and the chats on a paired computer (with a count of those that need you), then Models, Providers and Usage, and a profile row that opens Account. Those screens open over the chat with a back button. Home's releases and most used models are now the top of Models, and Compare opens from a model's "Compare with…" (and from the sidebar on tablets). Signed out, the app opens on Models with a "Sign in to chat" button; signing in opens a new chat. Old links, including `/`, and notification taps still open the right screen.
