The app opens on a new chat, and the bottom tab bar is gone. A sidebar (a drawer on phones that a drag anywhere on the chat opens, always open on tablets) holds search and New chat, Code (with a count of threads that need you), the chats on the phone, then Models, Providers and Usage, and a profile row that opens Account. Those screens open over the chat with a back button, and nothing opens twice. Code lists a paired computer's threads like the desktop's sidebar: needs you first, project folders with New thread, settled threads folded away; each thread opens as its own screen. The composer has one control for the model and its effort, with a sheet to pick both (favourites and recent models first); new threads on a computer can pick from the computer's models too. Home's releases and most used models are now the top of Models, and Compare opens from a model's "Compare with…" (and from the sidebar on tablets). Signed out, the app opens on Models with a "Sign in to chat" button; signing in opens a new chat. Old links, including `/`, and notification taps still open the right screen.
