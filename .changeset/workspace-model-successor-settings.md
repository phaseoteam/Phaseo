Add retired model routing settings to the workspace routing page: turn routing to replacement models on, choose whether a more expensive replacement may be used, and set your own replacement for a model or never redirect it. Workspace replacements need no admin approval and are used whatever they cost, but still wait for the 72-hour grace period and never turn a free model into a paid one.
