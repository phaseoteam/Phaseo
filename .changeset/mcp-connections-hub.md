Add Connections: connect remote MCP servers such as Linear, Notion or Sentry once in Settings > Connections, then use their tools from any MCP client through Phaseo's MCP server. Connections are personal, tokens are encrypted, calls run as the person who connected, and each call is recorded without its content. Off until MCP_HUB_ENABLED and the mcp_connections gate are turned on.
