Motion in the mobile app feels steadier and more physical. Bottom drawers on phones (confirmations, filters, provider details, option and action menus) now rise into place, and a grabber at the top lets you flick or drag them closed, or springs them back if you let go early. Toasts slide up to fill the gap when one leaves instead of jumping, the last toast now plays its exit, and a toast can be swiped up to dismiss it.

Pressing and dragging on a chart moves its tooltip on the UI thread, so it keeps up with your finger even while an answer is streaming in. On ProMotion iPhones, animations can now run at up to 120 frames per second.
