Charts on model pages and the rankings page can be shared. A share button opens a dialog with a branded Phaseo image of the chart (light or dark, with the top 5, top 10 or chosen series, or one series highlighted) and lets people copy or download it, post it to X or LinkedIn, or copy a link. Shared links open the chart with the same settings and are tagged with UTM parameters per channel.
