ElevenLabs text-to-speech accepts any voice ID from your voice library (premade, shared, cloned or designed) on every model, and `response_format: "wav"` returns a WAV file instead of headerless PCM. GPT Image 2.6 accepts the `xhigh` and `max` quality levels and the GPT Image 2 size rules. LTX-2.5 Pro accepts 1440p, 4K and 48 fps.
