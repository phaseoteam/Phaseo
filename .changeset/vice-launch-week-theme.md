Add an opt-in "Vice" theme for the GTA VI launch week (18–20 November 2026). While it is offered, every theme picker shows a Vice option with its own icon; choosing it switches the site to a dusk palette with flamingo pink, lagoon teal and a sunset gradient on large titles, primary buttons and progress marks, plus a Vice version of the header wordmark. It is never on by default, visitors can switch back at any time, and it disappears after the window closes.
