Restyle the dashboard and chat hover cards around one layout: a title row, a muted meta line, an optional headline figure and label/value rows. One shared timestamp card replaces seven near-identical local/UTC/relative cards, model, provider and app cards drop their "View" button because the trigger is the link, and every trigger can take keyboard focus. The provider attempt card shows its status, attempt number, duration and upstream error; the balance and key-limit cards lead with the balance and the remaining allowance; the council's fused-source card summarises what that source contributed. The BYOK credential hint is now a field description, and the chat's "+N" models overflow opens on click.
