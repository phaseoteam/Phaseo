Bill gateway server tools for what they cost. Advisor, subagent, Fusion and image-generation calls are priced on the called model's own route and added as separate pricing lines, and a paid called model needs wallet credit even behind a free main model. Phaseo's own web search ($0.007, plus $0.001 per result past 10) and managed web fetch ($0.001) are billed on every price card that does not set its own price, on streamed and buffered responses alike, and at cost on top of the BYOK fee for BYOK requests; failed searches, TinyFish searches and direct fetches stay free, and paid engines need wallet credit. Each round of a multi-round server-tool request is billed on the route and key it ran on, success or failure; a round whose route has no price card is not charged and is recorded for reconciliation, and a stealth model's routes stay out of its pricing lines. One HTTP request, model fallbacks included, counts once against the monthly BYOK allowance. A delivered response whose wallet charge fails, single- or multi-round, streamed or buffered, is recorded as pending reconciliation (never as charged) and its expected amount counts against spend limits; a wallet refusal or missing wallet is not retried and is recorded as `wallet_refused` or `wallet_not_found`; pending rows store the expected amount in `gateway_requests.expected_cost_nanos`, and a charged, delivered response counts against spend limits even if its log row cannot be written. A server-tool request that fails after completed rounds is charged for those rounds and their tool calls, logged with that cost and counted against spend limits; no tool runs past the last follow-up turn. An Anthropic Messages `max_tokens: 0` cache pre-warm returns its empty answer as a success and is billed for its cache writes, and a zero-output text response is otherwise waived only when it carried no billable input or cache writes. Workspaces can enable at most 3 data-contribution classifiers, enforced atomically in the database.
