On a computer's threads and new-thread screen, the phone's composer is one surface with nothing under it: the model sits beside send with its logo, name and effort (the name truncates first), and opens the thread's settings, which hold the model and effort, the mode (Chat, Code or Plan) and the read-only permissions. A new thread picks Local or a new worktree under its project. Phaseo chats show the model's name and effort beside send too. Pressed while typing, the model closes the keyboard before its sheet opens, so the sheet opens full height.
