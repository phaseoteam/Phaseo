The gateway can generate video with Gemini Omni on Google AI Studio through Google's Interactions API, from text, a first frame and optional last frame, reference images, or a source video. Videos are 3 to 10 seconds at 360p, 720p, 1080p or 4K, in 16:9 or 9:16, always with audio. They are billed on the tokens Google reports: video output, thinking and input.
