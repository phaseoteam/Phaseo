A paired phone with Use terminals on can open a thread's terminals on its computer, run the project's scripts, type in them (with Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, arrows and Paste), add chosen lines of output to the thread's message, and stop the local servers the thread started. The phone wraps at its own width and never resizes the window's terminal, and the window's title names the phone on every terminal it's in. Use terminals needs Read code, and stays off until it's turned on for that phone in Settings → Phone.
