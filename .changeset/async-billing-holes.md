Always charge finished batch and video jobs, into overdraft when the hold and balance do not cover the actual cost. Hold batch rows for every requested choice (`n`, `best_of`, `candidateCount`), and reject choice counts that are not whole numbers from 1 to 128. When a client disconnects from a stream that is still generating, charge an estimate of the output streamed so far 15 and 25 seconds after the disconnect (input is counted once the provider reports it; BYOK requests are skipped), so a drain stopped by the runtime is still billed. The final bill settles the request's total and refunds any estimate above it, and a stream whose upstream fails or resets is not billed. Spend limits count the final total once, when the request settles, including charged cancellations and failures, and count a charge the wallet confirmed even when the request row could not be written. A delivered request whose charge did not go through is recorded as pending reconciliation with its expected cost. Self-serve refund claims count every charge on a top-up as usage of it, once, at the time of the debit.
