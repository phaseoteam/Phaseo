On phones, model page provider cards show at most three prices on the first row and Latency, Throughput and Uptime on their own row below. Text models show Input, Output and Cache read. Image, video and audio models lead with their main output price. Uptime shows a labelled strip of the last three days instead of hover-only bars, and video resolution labels are hidden to keep the price row short.
