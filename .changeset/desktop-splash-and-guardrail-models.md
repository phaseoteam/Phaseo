Desktop opens on the Phaseo logo until your accounts have loaded, instead of a blank window and a sign-in card that vanishes a moment later. The window also appears sooner, as the MCP library now loads after it. Your signed-in email is blurred until clicked in the account menu, Settings → Accounts and Phaseo Connect. Phaseo's model list follows your workspace's guardrails: models and providers you've disallowed no longer appear.
