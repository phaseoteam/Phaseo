Removing someone from a workspace now revokes the API keys and management keys they created there, and the gateway drops its cached copies right away. Only owners and admins see the people who use an OAuth app, with each person's name and email shown only if they shared them. When stored contents are limited to owners and admins, web search queries and fetched URLs are hidden from other members too. Public presets no longer expose unpublished drafts. Only workspace owners can invite admins, and invite codes are generated by the server.
