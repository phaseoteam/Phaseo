Threads on a paired computer can be organised from the phone: swipe to settle, snooze or archive, a long-press menu with pin, rename and mark unread, undo, and an archive screen. A thread's mode, model and effort change from its floating composer, its queue can be sent now, edited, moved or removed, and each turn shows the files it changed.
