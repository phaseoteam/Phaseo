<Update label="February 27, 2026">

<div data-changelog-category="product">

<p className="changelog-category">Product</p>

- Email sign-in shows why an attempt failed, such as a wrong password or an unverified email, on the sign-in page instead of a generic error page ([#116](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/116)).
- Email sign-up lists the password rules (lowercase, uppercase, number and symbol) and checks them before the form is sent ([#113](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/113)).
- The models table shows prices per million units correctly and uses the full page width; before, some rows showed extremely high prices ([#113](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/113), [#116](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/116)).

</div>

<div data-changelog-category="api">

<p className="changelog-category">API & SDKs</p>

</div>

</Update>
