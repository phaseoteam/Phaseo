<Update label="August 31, 2026">

<div data-changelog-category="product">

<p className="changelog-category">Product</p>

- BYOK now includes 250,000 requests per workspace each UTC month without a Phaseo fee, then charges 2.5% of the provider-equivalent cost; request-level BYOK metadata is kept for 90 days ([#2056](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2056)).

<Update label="August 30, 2026">

<div data-changelog-category="product">

<p className="changelog-category">Product</p>

- Chat errors now say what went wrong and offer a next step, such as retry, sign in or pick another model; `402` errors link to Credits and `phaseo/free` ([#2100](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2100)).
- The Chat composer stops growing at five lines and scrolls long prompts instead of covering the conversation ([#2096](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2096)).
- Model pages open a provider's details from a link such as `?provider=openai`, and the provider dialog shows a copyable provider-qualified model ID; see [Provider-qualified model IDs](../guides/provider-qualified-models.mdx) ([#2095](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2095), [#2052](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2052)).
- Model search shows only models you can route through the gateway until you change the status filter; see [Check Gateway Reliability and Status](../developers/reliability-and-status.mdx) ([#2057](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2057)).
- The public Apps rankings group each website into one entry with a readable URL, such as `/apps/phaseo-chat` ([#2087](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2087)).
- The Trust Centre adds a security whitepaper, a subprocessor list and a draft DPA, and account deletion now removes data from every active Phaseo store within 30 days ([#2058](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2058)).

</div>

- The management API manages provider credentials at `/v1/byok` and workspace budgets at `/v1/budgets`, and every SDK is regenerated to match; see [List provider credentials](../api-reference/endpoint/provider-credentials-list.mdx) ([#2086](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/2086)).

- `POST /v1/audio/transcriptions` accepts OpenAI's diarized response formats, `chunking_strategy` and known-speaker fields; see [Create a transcription](../api-reference/endpoint/audio-transcriptions.mdx) ([#1451](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/1451)).
