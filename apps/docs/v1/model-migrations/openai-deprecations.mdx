Find the OpenAI model or product you use, move to the replacement before the shutdown date, and validate it against your production prompts, tools, latency, and cost. Dates come from OpenAI's [API deprecations page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).

| Deprecated model family | Deprecation date | Shutdown date | Recommended replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `whisper-1`, `gpt-4o-transcribe`, `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe`, `gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize` | August 26, 2026 | February 26, 2027 | `openai/gpt-transcribe` or `openai/gpt-live-transcribe` |
| `gpt-realtime`, `gpt-audio`, `gpt-4o-audio`, `gpt-4o-realtime`, `gpt-realtime-mini`, `gpt-audio-mini`, `gpt-4o-mini-realtime`, `gpt-4o-mini-audio` | July 20, 2026 | January 20, 2027 | `openai/gpt-realtime-2.1`, `openai/gpt-realtime-2.1-mini`, or `openai/gpt-audio-1.5` |
| `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe-2025-03-20` | July 20, 2026 | January 20, 2027 | `openai/gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` |
| GPT-5 and o3 dated snapshots | June 11, 2026 | December 11, 2026 | `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`, `openai/gpt-5.6-terra`, or `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` |
| `gpt-image-1-mini`, `gpt-image-1.5`, `chatgpt-image-latest` | June 2, 2026 | December 1, 2026 | `openai/gpt-image-2` |
| Legacy GPT-3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4.1 nano, GPT-4o, o1, o3-mini, and o4-mini snapshots | April 22, 2026 | October 23, 2026 | See the model-specific recommendations on OpenAI's deprecations page |
| Sora 2 models | March 24, 2026 | September 24, 2026 | No replacement announced |

The catalogue records these dates on the affected model entries, with a replacement model ID where there is one. The dates apply to the snapshots OpenAI names; Phaseo may serve a snapshot under a canonical model ID. A model's page shows its dates and suggested replacement, and Phaseo keeps serving a retired model while any provider does. See [Model Deprecation and Retirement](../guides/model-retirement) for alerts and automatic replacement.

## Platform deprecations

OpenAI also retires products that aren't models:

| Product | Date | Move to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reusable prompts | Shut down November 30, 2026 | Application code, or a Phaseo preset with [prompt templates](../guides/prompt-templates) |
| Evals platform | Read-only October 31, 2026; shut down November 30, 2026 | OpenAI recommends Promptfoo |
| Agent Builder | Shut down November 30, 2026 | The Agents SDK or ChatGPT Workspace Agents |

## Migrate

1. Replace the deprecated model or product in staging.
2. Re-run representative evaluations, especially structured outputs, tool calls, audio quality, and long-context tasks.
3. Compare latency and spend under production-like concurrency.
4. Canary the replacement with an explicit rollback route before the shutdown date.

For full snapshot mappings and endpoint-specific guidance, see [OpenAI's API deprecations documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).
