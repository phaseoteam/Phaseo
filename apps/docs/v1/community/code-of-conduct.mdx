Phaseo follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in GitHub issues and pull requests, Discord, and every other community space. Be kind, respectful and open to other viewpoints.

Report harassment or other unacceptable behavior to `support@phaseo.app` or to a moderator in [Discord](https://discord.gg/aQyywCvgZ5). Reports are reviewed by the community leaders, who keep the reporter's identity private.

The full text, including examples and enforcement steps, is in [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Contribution rules are in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
