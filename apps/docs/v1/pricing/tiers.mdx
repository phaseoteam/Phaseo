# Pricing Tiers

Gateway billing has two parts:

1. **Model usage pricing**: credits are consumed at model prices shown in the catalog.
2. **Credit purchase fee**: a flat **5% top-up fee** is applied only when buying credits.

## Current Plan

<Card title="Standard Plan" icon="tag">
  **5% credit purchase fee**

  Standard workspaces use pay-as-you-go Gateway pricing. Enterprise workspace add-ons are available for teams that need identity and directory controls.
</Card>

## How Billing Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy credits">
    When you top up, a **5% platform fee** is applied to the purchase.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the Gateway">
    Requests consume credits at the model prices listed in the catalog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review usage">
    Your dashboard shows the current top-up fee, credit balance, and recent spend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example

| Scenario | Total Charge | Applied Fee | Credits Added |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard plan | $100 | 5% (~$4.76) | ~$95.24 credits |
| Free model usage | No top-up | $0 | Uses free model allowance |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are fees charged on every request?">
    No. Fees are charged when you purchase credits. Requests consume those credits at the model prices displayed in the catalog.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you mark up token pricing beyond model rates?">
    Model usage rates follow the catalog pricing. The separate platform fee is the 5% top-up fee applied when credits are purchased.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there an enterprise plan?">
    Yes. Self Serve Enterprise starts at **$29/month for up to 100 workspace members**, including smaller teams, and costs **$79/month at 1,000 members**. SSO, SCIM, directory management, departments, and priority email support are included. Model credits are purchased separately with the standard 5% top-up fee ($1 minimum). Open **Pricing** in the dashboard to choose a member allowance and see the current quote, where self-service is available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which members count towards the Enterprise allowance?">
    Members count while they belong to the workspace, whether or not they sign in. The entry subscription includes up to 100 members. Below 100,000 members, increase your allowance before adding members beyond it. Above 100,000, additional unique members are billed at $0.02 per member per subscription billing period, including members removed during that period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request custom commercial terms?">
    Yes. For invoicing, procurement, or bespoke commercial requirements, contact sales. Standard Gateway usage remains available without an Enterprise add-on.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Viewing Your Pricing

Check your current top-up fee and billing status in your dashboard:

1. Navigate to **Settings -> Pricing & Tiers**
2. View the current fee rate and recent usage
3. Review credit balance and spend trends
