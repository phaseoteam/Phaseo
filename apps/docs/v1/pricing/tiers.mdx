Buy credits once and spend them on requests at the catalog price of each model. Phaseo's only fee on managed requests is 5% of each credit purchase, with a $1 minimum; requests themselves carry no markup.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  buy["Buy $100 of credits"] -->|"Pay $105<br/>(5% fee)"| wallet["Workspace wallet<br/>$100.00"]
  wallet -->|"Each request costs<br/>the model's catalog price"| spend["Balance goes down"]
```

## What a top-up costs

The fee is the larger of 5% of the credits you buy and $1. It is added to the amount you pay.

| Credits you buy | Fee | You pay |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $5.00 | $1.00 (minimum) | $6.00 |
| $20.00 | $1.00 | $21.00 |
| $100.00 | $5.00 | $105.00 |
| $1,000.00 | $50.00 | $1,050.00 |

Put the other way round, a $100.00 charge adds $95.24 of credits and a $4.76 fee. Dashboard top-ups start at $5.

Buy credits in **Settings -> Billing -> Billing & Credits**, or from a terminal with `phaseo credits add --amount <usd>` (see the [quickstart](../quickstart#add-credits-for-paid-models)). The same page shows your balance, the current fee and recent spend; **Settings -> Billing -> Transactions** lists each purchase.

## What doesn't use credits

| Usage | Phaseo charge |
| --- | --- |
| Free models (`phaseo/free` and models priced at $0) | None. Free-model requests have a daily allowance; see [Plans](../exploring/subscription-plans). |
| Bring your own key (BYOK) | No top-up. The first 250,000 completed BYOK requests per workspace each UTC month have no service fee, then 2.5% of the provider-equivalent cost. Your provider bills the model usage. See [BYOK considerations](../guides/routing-and-fallbacks#byok-considerations). |

## Enterprise

Self Serve Enterprise is a separate monthly subscription for SSO, SCIM, directory management, departments and priority email support. It starts at $29/month for up to 100 workspace members and is $79/month at 1,000 members. It doesn't include credits or change the 5% fee ($1 minimum). [Plans](../exploring/subscription-plans#enterprise-member-pricing) has the full member pricing and how members are counted.

For invoicing, procurement or custom commercial terms, contact the [Help Center](https://phaseo.app/help). Standard Gateway usage doesn't need Enterprise.
