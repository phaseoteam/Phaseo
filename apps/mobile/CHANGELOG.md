### Minor Changes

- [#3477](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3477) [`85f6240`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/85f6240a61eca896f3f340a6912d0c88f1b92953) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - Phone access: pair the phone app with the desktop by QR code and use your chats from the phone, directly over Tailscale or the same Wi-Fi, or from anywhere with Phaseo Connect. - [#3477](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3477) [`85f6240`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/85f6240a61eca896f3f340a6912d0c88f1b92953) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - The app opens on a new chat, and the bottom tab bar is gone. A sidebar (a drawer on phones that a drag anywhere on the chat opens, always open on tablets) holds search and New chat, Code (with a count of threads that need you), the chats on the phone, then Models, Providers and Usage, and a profile row that opens Account. Those screens open over the chat with a back button, and nothing opens twice. Code lists a paired computer's threads like the desktop's sidebar: needs you first, project folders with New thread, settled threads folded away; each thread opens as its own screen. The composer has one control for the model and its effort, with a sheet to pick both (favourites and recent models first); new threads on a computer can pick from the computer's models too. Home's releases and most used models are now the top of Models, and Compare opens from a model's "Compare with…" (and from the sidebar on tablets). Signed out, the app opens on Models with a "Sign in to chat" button; signing in opens a new chat. Old links, including `/`, and notification taps still open the right screen.

- [#3477](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3477) [`85f6240`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/85f6240a61eca896f3f340a6912d0c88f1b92953) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - Chats and Code threads share one composer. It sits on one line beside its controls until the text wraps, then grows above them to four lines; the model shows its logo, and its name and effort once there's room. With nothing typed, send turns into stop while answers run. In chats, messages sent while answers run wait in a queue that sends each one when the answers before it finish; queued messages can be edited or removed, and a stopped or failed answer pauses the queue until you resume it. Typing `/` offers model, compare, settings and usage. In Code threads, send steers the running turn, and a long press queues the message on the computer instead; approvals and questions show as cards over the composer, and New thread uses the same composer.

- [#3619](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3619) [`fd6a06b`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/fd6a06bfd48d94d871fc035d8a6ee03f5e4cd02a) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - Motion in the mobile app feels steadier and more physical. - [#3556](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3556) [`5c4c012`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/5c4c012f065fb7d63e14c32945602a4e0ec21592) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - The mobile model page shows provider uptime the way the website does. - [#3569](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/pull/3569) [`05a6934`](https://github.com/phaseoteam/Phaseo/commit/05a69345489af8456bc13b864e84daa580d55f24) Thanks [@DanielButler1](https://github.com/DanielButler1)! - Model uptime headline figures (Reachable, Accepted and Completed) are now the true share of all provider attempts in the last 24 hours, not an average of 24 hourly rates. The average counted idle hours as 100%, so a model could read "Completed 96.7%" while two in three of its accepted attempts failed. The failure share in each explanation comes from the same rates, so the figure and its explanation always agree. With no attempts in 24 hours, every step reads 100% with "No failures". The chart lines still show hours without data at 100%. The mobile app matches.
  
  Metric explanations on model pages open on tap and click as well as hover, and from the keyboard. This covers the three uptime labels and the Performance charts' help icons. The provider table's daily uptime bars now show their day and rate on hover, focus or tap, and a day with no traffic says so instead of "--".
  
  The explanations are more precise. Reachable now lists 502, 504, Cloudflare 520–527 and 503s without an overload signal, matching how the gateway classifies attempts. Accepted lists 429s on Phaseo's own key, 529s and overloaded responses. The failure lines are shorter, for example "66.7% failed after the provider accepted them". The Uptime column header explains what the 3-day uptime measures. Throughput, Latency and E2E latency now use the documented definitions: effective throughput, time to first token and full request time.
