# Low-cost platform observability

Build operator visibility across gateway requests, provider attempts, web APIs, browser/server errors and scheduled jobs. Supply versioned Grafana dashboards, alert rules, validation, provisioning and runbooks. Collection must be explicitly enabled with configured credentials, bounded, privacy-safe and independent of request/billing success. Deploy through the PR flow to the existing Cloudflare Workers. Keep the account on Free and preserve customer Broadcast and database behavior.

Acceptance: deterministic local transport and lifecycle tests; successful type/build checks for touched surfaces where the existing workspace permits; dashboard/alert schemas and queries match emitted events; missing telemetry and quiet traffic are distinct; no secrets committed. Grafana account delivery and source-map matching require a configured staging stack and are reported separately.
